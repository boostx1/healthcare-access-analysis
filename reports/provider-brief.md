# Brief for a provider partnerships team

## Which comparisons belong in a partner discussion?

Within the 2014–2023 study window, use 2022 for a common-year comparison of the six countries. In 2023 only 2 of the 6 countries had both measures. Mixing the newest value from each country would put different reporting years side by side.

| 2022 national indicator | United Arab Emirates | Sweden | Six-country median |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Hospital beds per 1,000 people | 1.87 | 1.90 | 2.17 |
| Out-of-pocket share of current health spending | 9.72% | 13.13% | 12.96% |

The two countries reported similar bed supply relative to population. Their household financing shares differed by 3.41 percentage points, with the higher share in Sweden. These shares describe how each health system was financed and cannot be converted into a price for an international patient.

Across the six countries, bed supply ranged from 1.25 in Qatar to 2.61 in Finland. Between 2015 and 2022, the first and last years with complete six-country coverage, UAE bed density changed by +0.44 beds per 1,000 and its out-of-pocket share changed by -8.07 percentage points. The indicators alone cannot explain those changes.

## What to ask the provider

For a partner offering a particular procedure, request staffed beds for the service, occupied bed-days and available staffed bed-days over the same month, and the median time from referral to treatment. For price comparisons, request the same procedure and inclusion criteria plus what an international patient must pay directly. These inputs would allow a follow-up analysis of provider capacity, delays and prices.

## Reading the figures

The median gives each selected country equal weight. These six countries are a purposive comparison group. National bed definitions and service delivery models can differ. Neither measure captures patient outcomes, quality, occupancy or the availability of an appointment. The inputs contain public, annual national aggregates.
